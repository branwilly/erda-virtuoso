FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. Your recordings stay on your iPad unless you send a take to a teacher through your own iCloud account. In the beta, pictures of the bars you answer in “Check this bar” are sent to the developer; you can switch this off in About Erda.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a take, excerpt or lesson in the app. Deleting the app removes the rest. To remove bar pictures already sent, email branwilly+erda@mail.grokbot.com with the date and your app version.

Does it include sheet music?

No. Bring music you own.
