Erda Virtuoso privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026.

Erda is an iPad app that helps musicians prepare for auditions. It is made by Brandon Williams. In short: your music, recordings and notes stay on your iPad. Erda has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The one exception, for people who join the beta, is small pictures of bars Erda wasn't sure about; it is described below.

What stays on your iPad

  • Recordings you make: practice takes, lessons and spoken notes.
  • Music you bring in: PDFs and .4sc score files, and the excerpts cut from them.
  • What you add: marks, notes, ratings, plans, lists, lesson transcripts and notes canvases.

All of this is kept in the app's own storage on your iPad. Erda doesn't sync it to iCloud or send it to us. It is included in backups of your iPad that you make with iCloud Backup or a computer, as other apps' data is.

The microphone

  • Erda uses the microphone when you record a take, a lesson or a spoken note.
  • If you turn on listening, Erda also listens while an excerpt is open, so every rep is kept. You can turn this off at any time. It stops when Erda is in the background, and a dot shows while it's on.
  • Talking between reps, and before or after you play, is recognised on your iPad (with Apple's built-in sound recognition) and left out of your takes.
  • Turning speech into text (lesson transcripts, spoken notes) happens on your iPad with Apple's speech recognition.
  • If you record a lesson, please ask your teacher first. Erda reminds you on screen.

What goes online, and when

  • About this piece: when you open it, Erda looks the piece up by sending only the composer's and the work's names to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) and to OpenAlex (OurResearch), an open index of published research. These services see your iPad's internet address, as any website does, and their own privacy policies apply. Nothing about you, your playing or your library is sent.
  • Erda's notes on a piece are written by Apple Intelligence: on your iPad, or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so that what it processes isn't stored or seen by Apple. Only the public Wikipedia text is given to it.
  • Talk with Erda: Erda answers from your practice on your iPad. Where Apple Intelligence is available, your question and a short summary of your practice (your auditions and their dates, today's plan, how your excerpts are going, recent ratings and your teachers' notes) are given to Apple's model, on your iPad or on Private Cloud Compute, which Apple designed so that what it processes isn't stored or seen by Apple. No recordings or music are sent.
  • Links to YouTube, Reddit and web searches open in their own app or browser only when you tap them.
  • Purchases, if Erda offers them, are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sending takes to your teachers

  • Everyone uses their own iCloud account. A student is signed in to their own Apple Account on their own device; a teacher is signed in to their own Apple Account on theirs. Nobody signs in to anyone else's account, and Erda never asks for an Apple password.
  • If you choose Send to your teachers, the take's recording, the excerpt's name, your rating and your note are saved in your own iCloud account and shared, through Apple's iCloud sharing, only with the teachers you invite. Every teacher you invite sees the takes you send and each other's replies.
  • Teachers' replies (comments, ratings and voice replies) are saved in the same shared space and come back to your iPad.
  • We (Erda's developer) can't see any of it: it's stored by Apple under your account. Stop sharing, or remove a teacher, at any time from Send to your teachers › Invite or remove teachers.
  • If you're a teacher, what students send you, and your replies, stay in their iCloud spaces; Erda on your iPhone shows them.

Bars Erda wasn't sure about

  • When Erda reads how a piece is counted, it marks bars it isn't sure of with "Check this bar". Your answers (the meter, free time, a tapped tempo) are saved with that page of music on your iPad.
  • When you join the beta, you agree that Erda sends a small picture of each bar you check, with your answer, to Erda's developer to help Erda learn. It sends only that: no name, no email, no recording, and nothing else from your page or library. It is stored in the developer's own iCloud container (Apple's CloudKit), which adds only an anonymous record number. The pictures are small pieces of music from the pages you opened.
  • You agree once, on the first screen after the welcome. You can stop it at any time in About Erda › Bars Erda wasn't sure about: nothing more is sent and anything waiting is deleted. To ask us to delete bars already sent, write to branwilly+erda@mail.grokbot.com; because they carry no name, tell us the date and your Erda version.

When something leaves your iPad because you chose to share it

  • Sharing a take, a page with marks, a list as a PDF, or crop corrections uses the iPad's share sheet. You choose where it goes.
  • If your library ever fails to open, "Send to support" makes a file with the library's records and a description of the problem, without your recordings, and you choose whether and how to send it.

Students, teachers and schools

  • Erda is made by an individual developer, not a school, and has no server that holds your recordings or comments. When a student sends a take to a teacher, it stays in the student's own iCloud account (see above). Because takes, ratings and comments between a student and a teacher can be part of a student's education record, teachers using Erda are responsible for keeping what they receive within their school's rules, including FERPA in the United States.
  • Erda asks you to agree before it first records, and reminds you to ask before recording lessons or other people.

Children

Erda is meant for people aged 13 and over.

Deleting your data

Delete anything inside Erda, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPad. Deleting a take, an excerpt, a lesson, a version or a piece removes its recording, pictures, music file and records from your iPad for good (a piece's PDF stays only while an excerpt cut from it still needs it), and a copy you sent to a teacher is removed from your iCloud too. If your iPad is offline at the time, the removal is sent the next time Erda is open with a connection.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page with a new date. Questions: branwilly+erda@mail.grokbot.com.
